Powerful features

More than just the weather

Everything you need to stay informed about the weather, without leaving your desktop.

🌡️

Current conditions

Temperature, feels-like, wind, humidity, pressure, UV index and much more.

📅

Detailed forecasts

Hourly and daily forecasts with customizable graphs and weather details.

🌧️

Interactive radar

Explore precipitation and weather patterns on an interactive map.

📈

Weather history

Explore past weather conditions and historical data.

🌍

Multiple providers

Choose from various weather services to get the data you need.

🎨

Your way

Customize the layout, forecast details, graphs and widget appearance.

🔒

Your privacy matters

The widget does not collect telemetry or track users. Your location is used to retrieve weather information from the selected weather provider. No advertising. No tracking.
